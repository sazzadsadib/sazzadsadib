### About

I like taking an idea that’s still a little messy and figuring out how to make it real. Sometimes that means building something useful; other times it means finding the right way to tell a story. I enjoy both the problem-solving and the creative decisions along the way.

For me, a good project starts with understanding what someone wants to achieve. I care about the small details, staying open to feedback, and making something that feels right for the people it’s meant for. If you have a project in mind—or an idea you’d like to think through together—I’d love to hear about it.
